About the Portal

Portal Purpose

The CCS Student Portal provides a single, organized web platform where students can quickly locate verified department updates and course materials without sifting through scattered group chats.

Intended Users

Built primarily for undergraduate Computing students, laboratory instructors, and academic advisers within the college department.

Accessibility Implementation

This portal implements high-contrast color pairings (exceeding WCAG AA 4.5:1 standards), semantic landmarks (<header>, <nav>, <main>, <footer>), descriptive alt attributes, a keyboard skip-to-content link, and high-visibility :focus-visible outlines for keyboard navigation.
